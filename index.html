The House · Accra

Chosen slowly.
Kept for years.

CASWISS is an independent curator of timepieces and leather in Accra. We look for pieces with a clear point of view — good bones, honest materials and a story that holds up in person. Then we tell you everything we know.

No pressure, no theatre. Just a private conversation, a close look and the time to decide.
